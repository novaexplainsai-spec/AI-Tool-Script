## Hook
Imagine you're in the middle of a task that involves AI agents, and they suddenly stop to ask for your approval. You have to drop everything, open your terminal, and type in a command to let them proceed. This can be frustrating, especially if you're in a hurry or not at your desk. That's where Pushary comes in - a tool that lets you approve AI requests right from your lock screen.

## Context
Pushary is designed for developers and power users who work with AI agents like Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI, Hermes, or Claude Cowork. If you're someone who uses these tools regularly, you know how annoying it can be to have to constantly switch between your terminal and your task at hand. In this video, we'll take a look at how Pushary works, its features, and how it can streamline your workflow. We'll cover the setup process, the mobile apps, and the audit trail, so you can decide if Pushary is right for you.

Pushary's tagline, "Approve AI requests from your lock screen," sums up its main benefit. With Pushary, you can connect your AI agents to your lock screen with just one command. When an AI agent stops to ask for approval, you can simply tap "yes" on your phone, and the task will continue running. This can save you a lot of time and reduce the frustration of having to constantly switch between your terminal and your task.

One of the key features of Pushary is its native iPhone and Android apps. These apps allow you to pair your device with your terminal using a QR code, eliminating the need to paste a key. This makes the setup process quick and easy. Additionally, Pushary's per-tool policies auto-approve safe reads, which means you don't have to worry about approving every single request manually.
